USDA lookups — worth setting up

      
Add a key and scanning gets noticeably better.
        USDA FoodData Central stores the nutrition panel exactly as printed on
        the packet, so a scan needs no guessing at all. It is asked alongside
        Open Food Facts on every scan and its answer wins whenever it has one.

      
The key is free, takes about thirty seconds, and stays on
        this phone - it is only ever sent to USDA, and is never committed to
        the repository. Without one the app still works: Open Food Facts covers
        more products, it just often lacks the serving size.

      
      
      
      

      
Free keys come from
        fdc.nal.usda.gov.

    


    

      
Check my foods

      
Goes through everything in your library looking for
        mistakes: calories that do not match the macros, portions that are the
        wrong size, a barcode on two foods. It can also re-ask the databases
        about anything you scanned and tell you where your figures differ from
        theirs. It only reports - nothing is changed.

      
      
      

      

    


    

      
Backup

      
Your data lives in this browser only. If you clear site
        data or switch phones it is gone, so export a copy now and then.
